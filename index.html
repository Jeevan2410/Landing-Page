Questions

Does it work with Android and iPhone?

In this concept, yes: it pairs over Bluetooth with either and syncs health data to the phone's own health app.

Can I swim with it?

It is water resistant to 50 metres, so pool and open-water swims are fine. Not for diving.

Which size should I pick?

41 mm suits wrists up to about 17 cm; 45 mm has a larger screen and a slightly bigger battery.

Is this a real product?

No. Halcyon One is a concept made to show web design and 3D motion. Nothing on this page is for sale and no payment is taken.
